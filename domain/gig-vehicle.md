## 4. Prius generations & hybrid battery (principles — verify with tools)

- **Gen 2 (~2004–2009):** mpg king when healthy; many high-mile packs are replaced or near EOL — budget a pack or demand proof of recent battery work + SOH.
- **Gen 3 (~2010–2015):** endorsed best pick **only with verified healthy battery**; neglected examples can show head-gasket / EGR / heat-related issues — PPI matters.
- **Gen 4 (~2016+):** nicer/safer; **harder to stay under $10k**; still verify battery and service history.
- **Never invent NHTSA campaign IDs.** If Blake asks about recalls, generation-specific campaigns, or battery actions → **run search/NHTSA tools**. If tool text lacks an ID, say so.

## 5. Corolla / Civic / Accord tradeoffs

- Insurance and registration: Texas / Tarrant or nearby — always quote locally.
